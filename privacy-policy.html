Privacy Policy for Ties

Last updated: September 27, 2026

TL;DR: Ties is a privacy-first app. Your contacts and notes stay on your device, encrypted, and are never sent to us. We run no servers, show no ads and track nothing. The only data that leaves your device relates to a purchase, and only if you buy Pro.

1. Overview

Ties is a personal relationship manager designed with privacy as the core principle. We believe your personal contacts and relationships are yours alone.

2. Data Storage

  • Local Only: All data is stored exclusively on your device
  • Encrypted: Your database is encrypted with AES-256 using SQLCipher
  • No Cloud: We do not operate any servers or cloud storage
  • No Transmission: Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly create a backup

3. Data Collection

We collect no personal data. Your contacts, interactions, notes and reminders are never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

  • ❌ No analytics
  • ❌ No crash reporting
  • ❌ No usage tracking
  • ❌ No location data
  • ❌ No advertising, no ad networks, no profiling

The only exception is purchase processing: if you buy Pro, RevenueCat and Google handle the transaction and we receive a confirmation of your purchase status. See sections 6 and 7 for details.

About the advertising ID: our payment provider RevenueCat includes Google's advertising ID library, which it may use to attribute a purchase to the right account and to detect fraudulent transactions. Ties itself never reads this identifier, and it is never used for advertising, profiling or tracking you across apps. You can reset or delete it at any time in your Android settings under Privacy → Ads.

4. Permissions

The app requests these permissions for local functionality:

  • Contacts: To import contacts from your device (optional, only when you choose to import)
  • Notifications: To send you reminders to stay in touch
  • Storage: To save your encrypted database on your device

No permission is used to collect or transmit data.

5. Backups

Backups are created on your device only. Ties does not upload them anywhere.

  • Automatic daily backups are stored in the app's private storage on your device
  • You can export your data manually as a JSON file
  • An exported file leaves the app only if you choose to share or move it — for example to your own cloud storage. From that point the file is governed by whichever service you send it to
  • Exported files are not encrypted. The database on your device is, but an export is plain JSON so that you can read and restore it without Ties. Store it somewhere you trust
  • You can disable automatic backups at any time in Settings

6. Payment Processing

In-app purchases are processed by RevenueCat and Apple/Google:

  • We only receive anonymized purchase receipts to verify your Pro status
  • Your payment information is handled exclusively by Apple/Google
  • We do not store credit card or payment details

7. Third-Party Services

The app uses these services:

  • RevenueCat: in-app purchase management. Receives purchase receipts and an anonymous app user id, plus the advertising identifier described in section 3. It receives none of your contacts, notes or interactions.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play Store: App distribution and payments

No advertising networks, no analytics, and no cross-app tracking are used.

8. Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Uninstalling the app deletes all local data, including its backups
  • Any file you exported yourself can be deleted wherever you saved it
  • We have nothing to delete on our end (because we don't store anything)

Purchase records held by RevenueCat and Google are tied to your store account. Contact us at the address in section 11 if you have questions about them.

9. Children's Privacy

Ties is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect app changes. The "Last updated" date will be revised. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
