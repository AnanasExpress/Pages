Privacy Policy for Ties

Last updated: July 27, 2026

TL;DR: Ties is a privacy-first app. All your data stays on your device, encrypted. We don't collect, track, or share anything. Period.

1. Overview

Ties is a personal relationship manager designed with privacy as the core principle. We believe your personal contacts and relationships are yours alone.

2. Data Storage

  • Local Only: All data is stored exclusively on your device
  • Encrypted: Your database is encrypted with AES-256 using SQLCipher
  • No Cloud: We do not operate any servers or cloud storage
  • No Transmission: Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly create a backup

3. Data Collection

We collect ZERO data.

  • ❌ No analytics
  • ❌ No crash reporting
  • ❌ No usage tracking
  • ❌ No location data
  • ❌ No device identifiers
  • ❌ No third-party SDKs (except payment processing)

4. Permissions

The app requests these permissions for local functionality:

  • Contacts: To import contacts from your device (optional, only when you choose to import)
  • Notifications: To send you reminders to stay in touch
  • Storage: To save your encrypted database on your device

No permission is used to collect or transmit data.

5. Backups

Current functionality:

  • Local Export: You can manually export your data as an encrypted JSON file stored on your device
  • Export files are encrypted with AES-256
  • You have full control over export files (can delete, move, or restore from them)

Planned feature (not yet available):

Future versions will offer optional automatic backups to your personal cloud storage (iCloud Drive on iOS, Google Drive on Android):

  • Backups will be encrypted before upload using AES-256
  • We will have no access to your cloud storage or backup files
  • Backups will go directly to your own iCloud/Google Drive account
  • You will control backup frequency and can disable it anytime

Until this feature is released, all backups are manual and local only.

6. Payment Processing

In-app purchases are processed by RevenueCat and Apple/Google:

  • We only receive anonymized purchase receipts to verify your Pro status
  • Your payment information is handled exclusively by Apple/Google
  • We do not store credit card or payment details

7. Third-Party Services

The app uses these services:

  • RevenueCat: In-app purchase management (only purchase validation, no personal data)
  • Apple App Store / Google Play Store: App distribution and payments

No advertising, analytics, or tracking services are used.

8. Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Uninstalling the app deletes all local data permanently
  • Manual export files can be deleted by you from your device storage
  • We have nothing to delete on our end (because we don't store anything)

When cloud backup is added in the future, you will be able to delete those backups from your iCloud/Google Drive.

9. Children's Privacy

Ties is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect app changes. The "Last updated" date will be revised. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
